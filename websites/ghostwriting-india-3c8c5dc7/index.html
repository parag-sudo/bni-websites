About Us

Ghostwriting India, owned and operated by Audit Think To Share, is a professional writing service based in Temecula dedicated to giving voice to stories that matter. We specialize in sensitive, deeply human narratives surrounding anxiety, depression, trauma, addiction, and women's and men's personal journeys.

Whether you need a polished manuscript, a compelling screenplay, or crisp marketing copy, our team crafts every word with care, confidentiality, and authenticity — so your message reaches the world exactly as you intend it.

Our Services

  • Copywriting — Persuasive, brand-driven content for websites, campaigns, blogs, and marketing materials that connect with your audience and convert.
  • Proofreading & Editing — Thorough line-by-line review for grammar, clarity, tone, and consistency to ensure your writing is flawless before it reaches readers.
  • Screenplay Writing — Original scripts and story development for film, television, and digital platforms, crafted with authentic characters and compelling narrative arcs.
  • Personal Narrative & Ghost Authorship — Confidential book writing and memoir services for individuals navigating anxiety, depression, trauma, addiction, and personal transformation.
