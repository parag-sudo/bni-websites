About Us

Ghostwriting India, owned by Audit Think To Share, is a dedicated writing service based in Temecula that helps individuals transform their ideas, experiences, and stories into powerful written works. We specialize in sensitive, meaningful narratives — including those that explore personal journeys through anxiety, depression, trauma, and addiction.

Whether you are a woman or man seeking to share your story, or a professional in need of polished content, we bring empathy, skill, and discretion to every project we take on.

Our Services

  • Copywriting: Compelling, clear, and persuasive content crafted for websites, marketing materials, blogs, and more — tailored to your brand and voice.
  • Proofreading: Professional review and correction of grammar, punctuation, structure, and clarity to ensure your writing is polished and publication-ready.
  • Screenplay Writing: From concept to final draft, we help bring your cinematic stories to life with well-structured scripts and authentic dialogue.
  • Personal Narratives & Ghostwriting: Sensitive and confidential storytelling for memoirs, personal essays, and books covering themes such as mental health, addiction recovery, trauma, and personal growth.
