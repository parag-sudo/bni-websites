About Us

Who We Are

Ghostwriting India, powered by Audit Think To Share, specializes in crafting authentic narratives around mental health, personal struggles, and transformative journeys. Based in Temecula, we combine expert copywriting, meticulous proofreading, and compelling screenplay writing to give voice to stories about anxiety, depression, trauma, addiction, and gender-specific wellness issues. Our mission is to help individuals and organizations communicate their most sensitive stories with clarity, compassion, and impact.
