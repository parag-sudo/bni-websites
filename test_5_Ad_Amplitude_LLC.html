About Us

Springfield's Most Trusted Ad Agency

Ad Amplitude, LLC is a full-service advertising and digital marketing agency proudly based in Springfield, MO. Founded and led by David Tibbles, our agency was built on a single belief: every business — regardless of size — deserves marketing that actually moves the needle. David's hands-on approach and deep expertise across digital channels ensure every campaign is crafted with both creative vision and strategic precision.

From nationally recognized brands to thriving local businesses, we've helped clients across the Ozarks region grow their revenue, expand their reach, and outpace their competition. Our team combines cutting-edge technology with proven advertising fundamentals to deliver outcomes that speak for themselves.

When you partner with Ad Amplitude, you gain a transparent, dedicated team that treats your marketing budget like it's their own — maximizing every dollar while keeping you informed every step of the way.
