Who We Are

Marketing That Actually Moves the Needle

Arcbound Marketing is a full-service advertising agency based in Chattanooga, TN, founded and led by Elijah Daves with a singular mission: to help businesses break through the noise and build brands that resonate. Elijah's hands-on approach and deep understanding of the Tennessee market make Arcbound a trusted partner for businesses of all sizes across the Scenic City.

What sets Arcbound apart is our unwavering commitment to measurable results. We don't just create eye-catching ads — we build comprehensive, strategy-first campaigns that connect the right message to the right audience at exactly the right moment. Our team combines creative firepower with analytical precision to deliver campaigns that genuinely drive growth.

Whether you're a local startup looking to make your mark or an established brand ready to scale, Arcbound Marketing has the expertise, tools, and passion to take your marketing from ordinary to extraordinary.
