About Us

The Agency Behind Oxnard's Growing Brands

Founded by Francisco De La Torre, Lider Media is a full-service advertising agency rooted in Oxnard, CA, with a passion for transforming local businesses into recognizable, powerful brands. Francisco built this agency on the belief that every business — no matter its size — deserves world-class marketing that delivers measurable growth.

With deep ties to the Oxnard community and Ventura County, Lider Media combines creative storytelling with strategic data analysis to craft advertising campaigns that truly resonate with target audiences. We stay ahead of industry trends so our clients always benefit from the most effective, modern marketing techniques available.

Our team of dedicated creatives, strategists, and media buyers work collaboratively to ensure every campaign exceeds expectations. Trusted by over 200 businesses, we are proud to be the agency Oxnard turns to when results matter most.
